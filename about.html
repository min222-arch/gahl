COMPANY PROFILE

Building Trust Through Quality Agriculture

Global Agri Harvest Ltd (GAHL) is a licensed Kenyan agricultural export company committed to supplying premium-quality horticultural products to international markets.

We specialize in fresh culinary herbs while strategically expanding into spices, fruits, vegetables and value-added agricultural products. Through innovation, sustainability and strong farmer partnerships, we bridge Kenyan agriculture with buyers across Europe, the Middle East, Asia and other global markets.
